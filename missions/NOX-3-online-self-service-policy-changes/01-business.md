# Business requirement: Online self-service policy changes

## The request
"As a policyholder viewing my policy, I want to submit change requests online so that my updates are processed directly without calling the contact centre."

## Problem
Today, customers who need to make routine updates to their policy—such as changing their home address, updating vehicle details, or adjusting cover—cannot do so online. When viewing their policy in their online account, they are told to phone customer support.

This creates significant friction and cost across the business:
- Every single policy adjustment requires manual phone handling by contact centre agents, creating long call queues and driving up operational support costs.
- Customers face unnecessary delays and frustration waiting on hold for simple updates they expect to complete online in minutes.
- Manual phone updates increase the risk of communication errors, such as billing notices continuing to go out to old mailing addresses.

## Who is affected
- **Policyholders**: Tens of thousands of active home and motor insurance customers who need to update their personal details, address, vehicle, or cover levels during their policy term.
- **Contact centre agents and team leads**: Support staff whose phone lines are tied up with repetitive administrative changes rather than complex customer queries and claims handling.

## What should change
- Policyholders viewing their policy in their online account will see clear options to submit change requests directly on screen (such as updating their address, updating vehicle details, or adjusting cover).
- Customers will enter their updated information through a simple online form and submit it immediately.
- Once submitted, the updated policy details will reflect in their account, and the customer will see an immediate confirmation that their change has been applied.
- Customers will no longer be directed to phone the contact centre for routine policy amendments.

## What "done" looks like
- A policyholder can sign in to their online account, update their address or policy details, and see the confirmed changes on screen without calling support.
- Contact centre phone queues show an immediate reduction in call volumes for routine policy adjustments.
- Operations reports confirm that online policy changes reflect accurately across customer records and billing communications.

## Examples
- **Example 1: Moving house (Address change)**  
  On 14 November, Sarah moves from 12 High Street to 45 Meadow Lane. She logs into her online account, opens her home insurance policy, enters her new address, and submits the change. Her policy immediately reflects 45 Meadow Lane as the insured and mailing address, and she sees a confirmation message on screen without waiting in a phone queue.

- **Example 2: Changing vehicle details**  
  On 3 December, David buys a new car and wants to update his active motor policy. Instead of calling customer support during work hours, he logs into his account in the evening, submits his new vehicle registration and details, and receives confirmation that his policy has been updated.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] A policyholder viewing their policy online can find and open a form to submit policy changes.
- [ ] Submitting an address or policy update online updates the policy details shown in the customer account immediately.
- [ ] The customer receives a clear on-screen confirmation upon submitting their change request without any prompt to call customer support.
- [ ] Contact centre call volume reports show a measurable drop in calls categorized as routine policy amendments.
- [ ] "As a policyholder viewing my policy, I want to submit change requests online so that my updates are processed directly without calling the contact centre."
