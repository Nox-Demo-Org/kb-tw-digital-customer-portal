## What the knowledge base says

The observed behaviour is intended per architectural decision record 'ADR: Claim Tracker Step Mapping & Handler Visibility' (customer-portal/decisions/adr-claim-tracker-step-mapping). In app/claims/[id]/page.tsx, the customer portal explicitly maps both 'submitted' and 'assigned' internal statuses to the customer-facing milestone 'Submitted' and omits handler details. Furthermore, notifications-hub intentionally does not subscribe to the claims.handler.assigned Pub/Sub event, explaining why 0 subscribers were reported and why customers receive no notification until claims advance to review.

- [[kb:customer-portal/decisions/adr-claim-tracker-step-mapping]]: The portal v1 design deliberately maps both 'submitted' and 'assigned' statuses to 'Submitted' and omits handler details from the customer view.
- [[kb:customer-portal/concepts/contact-centre-escalations]]: Hiding the handler assignment step causes 41% of claimants to call the contact centre asking 'where is my claim?' within five days.
- [[kb:claims-management/concepts/claim-lifecycle]]: When a handler is assigned, internal status moves to 'assigned' and claims.handler.assigned is published, but the customer-facing status remains 'Submitted' until assessment begins.
- [[kb:notifications-hub/entities/subscription-registry]]: notifications-hub explicitly does not subscribe to claims.handler.assigned, so no automated customer notification is sent when a handler is assigned.
