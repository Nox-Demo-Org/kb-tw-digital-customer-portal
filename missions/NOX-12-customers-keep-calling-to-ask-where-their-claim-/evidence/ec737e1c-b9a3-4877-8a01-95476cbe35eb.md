## What the knowledge base says

The behavior is intended per current design. According to ADR 'Claim Tracker Step Mapping & Handler Visibility' (adr-claim-tracker-step-mapping), customer-portal intentionally collapses internal 'assigned' status into the customer-facing 'Submitted' milestone and explicitly omits handler details in app/claims/[id]/page.tsx. Furthermore, notifications-hub explicitly ignores claims.handler.assigned, resulting in 0 subscribers and no automated customer message. This creates a documented 4.6-day average customer visibility blind spot between intake and active review that drives 41% of policyholders to call the contact centre asking about claim status. Improvements to expose handler assignment are tracked under Digital Claims v2.

- [[kb:customer-portal/decisions/adr-claim-tracker-step-mapping]]: The claim tracker explicitly maps both 'submitted' and 'assigned' statuses to 'Submitted' and omits handler details in the v1 implementation.
- [[kb:customer-portal/concepts/contact-centre-escalations]]: Collapsing 'assigned' into 'Submitted' causes 41% of claimants to call the contact centre within 5 days asking 'Where is my claim?'.
- [[kb:customer-portal/concepts/claim-tracking-flow]]: The tracker UI maps internal statuses via STEP_FOR, leaving claims in the 'Submitted' milestone until investigation advances to 'in_review'.
- [[kb:notifications-hub/summaries/api-spec]]: The notifications-hub explicitly excludes claims.handler.assigned from its subscriptions, sending no automatic customer alerts on assignment.
